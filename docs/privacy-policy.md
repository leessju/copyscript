# CopyScript Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** March 24, 2026

## Overview

CopyScript is a Chrome extension that copies YouTube video transcripts to your clipboard. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection

**CopyScript does NOT collect, store, or transmit any user data.** Specifically:

- No personal information is collected
- No browsing history is tracked
- No analytics or telemetry data is sent
- No cookies are set or read
- No external API calls are made
- No data leaves your browser

## How It Works

The extension operates entirely within your browser:

1. It reads the transcript panel DOM on YouTube video pages
2. It copies the transcript text to your local clipboard
3. No data is sent to any server

## Permissions Used

- **activeTab**: To access the current YouTube page DOM when you click the Copy button
- **youtube.com host permission**: To inject the Copy button into YouTube's interface

## Third-Party Services

CopyScript does not use any third-party services, APIs, or analytics tools.

## Changes

If this privacy policy changes, we will update this document with a new date.
